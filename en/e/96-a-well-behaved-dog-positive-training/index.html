Does your dog pull on the lead, jump up at visitors or wee in the wrong place? Teach with positive reinforcement, no punishment.

A Well-Behaved Dog is a practical guide to positive training for dog owners, with step-by-step instructions and short sessions.

You learn how dogs learn, how to prepare for training, teach sit, down, stay and come, walk without pulling, get the toilet spot right, handle biting, jumping up, barking and anxiety, and follow a 4-week plan.

What you’ll gain

  • Step-by-step basic cues
  • Toilet-training guide
  • Solutions for jumping up and barking
  • Guidance on anxiety and fears
  • A 4-week plan

This e-book is for educational purposes. It is not a substitute for advice from a vet, a dog trainer or an animal behaviour specialist. Sudden changes in behaviour can have a health cause: take your animal to the vet. In cases of aggression, consult a professional.
