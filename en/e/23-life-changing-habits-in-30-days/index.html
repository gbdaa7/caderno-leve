Start the year full of promises and by February there’s nothing left? The problem isn’t a lack of willpower, it’s starting too big.

Life-Changing Habits in 30 Days is a practical e-book in plain language for anyone who wants to build good habits that last and drop the ones that get in the way, without relying on motivation.

You’ll understand how habits form according to research and learn to choose the right habits, start with tiny versions, anchor them to your routine, use your environment to your advantage, replace bad habits and deal with setbacks, with a 30-day plan to build the foundation.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand the habit cycle and how long it takes
  • Choose concrete habits with a domino effect
  • Start with tiny habits that fit even bad days
  • Anchor new habits to the routine you already have
  • Use your environment to make the good easy and the bad hard
  • Track your progress with a 30-day calendar

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who always starts and gives up
  • Anyone who wants to change their routine with realistic steps
  • Anyone who wants to drop habits that get in the way

Who it’s not for

  • Doesn’t promise automatic habits in 30 days: the plan builds the foundation
  • Not a treatment for addictions (alcohol, tobacco, drugs)

Bonus: printable 30-day calendar — your habit recipe, a tracking calendar and a weekly checklist.
