Someone compliments you and you play it down straight away? You make a small mistake and spend the day calling yourself names? Real self-esteem isn’t thinking you’re perfect: it’s recognising your worth, flaws and all.

Real Self-Esteem is a practical, warm and straightforward e-book, with exercises to change the way you talk to yourself and stand up for yourself in everyday life.

No magic phrases in the mirror: these are concrete tools, like the evidence journal, the friend technique, the script for saying no and the courage ladder.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand what healthy self-esteem is (and what false self-esteem looks like)
  • Discover where the voice that puts you down comes from and learn to answer it
  • Recognise your strengths based on facts, not ready-made phrases
  • Stop putting yourself down: accept compliments and cut unnecessary apologies
  • Say no without guilt, with a simple script
  • Act even when you feel insecure, one step at a time

Who it’s for

  • People who are too hard on themselves and struggle to recognise their own worth
  • Anyone who says yes to everything for fear of displeasing others
  • Anyone who wants practical exercises to do at their own pace

Who it’s not for

  • It is not a treatment and does not replace psychotherapy or medical care
  • Not intended as the only resource for depression, eating disorders or abuse

Bonus: printable worksheet — a table for answering your inner critic, a one-week evidence journal and a daily checklist, plus a 21-day plan.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
