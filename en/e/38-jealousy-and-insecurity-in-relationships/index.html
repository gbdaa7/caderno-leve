Does one unanswered message send your mind spinning stories? You can deal with jealousy without controlling your partner or suffering so much.

Jealousy and Insecurity in Relationships is a practical e-book in plain language for anyone who feels too much jealousy and wants to live their relationship with more trust and ease.

You will understand what jealousy is, tell ordinary from possessive jealousy, discover its roots (such as attachment styles), question your thoughts, reduce checking, put together a calming kit, learn to talk from vulnerability and recognise when jealousy has turned into control.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand the emotions behind jealousy
  • Tell ordinary jealousy from possessive jealousy
  • Question the thoughts that feed mistrust
  • Break the checking cycle
  • Talk about insecurities without accusing
  • Strengthen self-esteem and trust

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who feels jealous and suffers because of it
  • Anyone who wants to stop watching their partner
  • Couples who want to talk better about insecurity

Who it’s not for

  • Not therapy or treatment
  • Not a guide for situations of abuse (where to find help is included)

Bonus: printable jealousy record — thoughts, evidence and postponed checks.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
