Does your child throw themselves on the floor and you end up shouting too? You can hold limits with firmness and kindness, without shouting.

Tantrums and Limits Without Shouting is a practical e-book in plain language for mums, dads and carers of young children who want to handle tantrums more calmly.

You will understand why tantrums happen, learn to prevent them with routine and choices, take the adult pause, acknowledge emotions while holding the limit, use logical consequences instead of physical punishment, strengthen connection with positive attention, and know what to do about tantrums in public and when to seek help.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand what lies behind tantrums
  • Prevent tantrums with routine, warnings and choices
  • Stay calm in the difficult moment
  • Acknowledge the emotion without giving up the limit
  • Use logical consequences, without hitting or humiliating
  • Know how to act during tantrums in public

Who it’s for

  • Parents and carers of children aged 1 to 7
  • Anyone who wants to parent without shouting or smacking
  • Grandparents, aunts, uncles and educators

Who it’s not for

  • Does not replace a paediatrician or child psychologist
  • Not permissiveness: firmness with kindness

Bonus: printable tantrum diary — a record of situations and your house rules.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
