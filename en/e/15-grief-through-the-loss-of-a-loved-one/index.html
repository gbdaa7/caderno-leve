Have you lost someone you love and feel the world has changed? There’s no right way to grieve, but you don’t have to go through it without support.

Grief: Through the Loss of Someone We Love is a comforting, practical e-book in plain language for anyone who has lost someone dear, and for anyone who wants to support a grieving person.

You’ll understand what often happens in the body and mind, learn what more recent research says about grief, and find ways to care for yourself in the early days, to ride out waves of longing and difficult dates, to deal with guilt and to keep your bond with the person who died, as well as guidance on talking to children and recognising when to seek help.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand the common reactions in grief and know you’re not alone
  • Clear up myths that create guilt and pressure
  • Look after yourself in the first days and weeks
  • Ride out waves of longing and plan for difficult dates
  • Deal with guilt, anger and what was left unsaid
  • Support other people and children who are grieving

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who has lost someone dear, recently or some time ago
  • Anyone who wants to support a grieving relative or friend
  • Anyone looking for comfort and reliable information

Who it’s not for

  • It does not replace psychotherapy, support groups or medical care
  • It does not impose deadlines or a right way to grieve

Bonus: Comfort sheet — space for memories, a support network and a self-care checklist, plus a guide to small acts of care.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
