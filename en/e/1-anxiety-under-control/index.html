Racing heart, a mind that won’t switch off, that tight feeling in your chest before a difficult conversation? You’re not alone — and there are simple tools that can help in everyday life.

Anxiety Under Control, Day by Day is a practical e-book, written in plain, warm language, to help you understand how anxiety works and learn self-care techniques that fit into your routine: in traffic, at work, before bed.

No complicated theory or miracle promises: just exercises, checklists and step-by-step guides you can start practicing today.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand what happens in your body and mind when anxiety shows up
  • Notice the early warnings, before tension reaches its peak
  • Use the S.T.O.P. method to pause before reacting
  • Practice 4 breathing techniques and several grounding techniques
  • Sort out your thoughts on paper and step out of “what if everything goes wrong?”
  • Build a routine that protects you and your own SOS kit

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who feels anxiety getting in the way of daily life and wants practical tools
  • Anyone who wants to complement the support they already receive with self-care exercises
  • Anyone who prefers a short, direct guide in plain language

Who it’s not for

  • It is not a treatment and does not replace therapy or medical care
  • It is not meant as the only resource for intense crises — in that case, please seek professional help

Bonus: printable worksheet and checklist — a personal SOS kit, a thought record table and a daily self-care checklist, plus a 14-day plan.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
