Revolving credit card balance, overdraft, overdue bills and that feeling the money never stretches far enough? You’re not alone — and you can get out of the chaos with a clear method.

A Plan to Get Out of Debt is a practical guide in plain language that helps you see all your debts, understand which weigh most, build a realistic budget and negotiate agreements you can afford — with no magic formulas and no promised timelines.

What you’ll gain

  • Take a complete X-ray of your debts, including forgotten ones
  • Understand interest and APR so you know which debts grow fastest
  • Build a survival budget and find out how much you can pay
  • Choose what to pay first (avalanche and snowball methods)
  • Negotiate safely using a ready-made conversation script
  • Know your rights (switching lenders, formal debt solutions) and avoid scams

  2. Chapter 2X-ray: list all your debts
  3. Chapter 3Understanding interest: which debts weigh most
  4. Chapter 4Survival budget

  6. Chapter 6Negotiation: an agreement you can afford

  10. ExtraSummary and action plan to put it into practice

Who it’s for

  • Anyone in debt who doesn’t know where to start
  • Anyone who has tried negotiating but fell behind again
  • Anyone who wants to organise household finances with a simple method

Who it’s not for

  • Not financial, legal or accounting advice
  • Does not promise payoff within a set time or guaranteed discounts

Bonus: printable sheets — debt table, survival budget, negotiation log and monthly checklist, plus a 30-day plan.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational purposes. It is not financial, legal or accounting advice and does not guarantee deadlines, discounts or results — every situation is different. Rules, rates and products vary by country and change over time. Before signing any agreement, check the terms carefully and, if needed, contact a consumer protection body, a free debt advice service or a professional you trust.
