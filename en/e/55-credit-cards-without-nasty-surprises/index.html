Does your card statement scare you every month? Learn to make your card work for you.

Credit Cards Without Nasty Surprises is an educational, practical guide to how cards work and how to avoid the highest interest rates on the market.

You’ll learn the dates that matter, the risks of revolving credit and piled-up instalments, a control routine, protection against scams and a plan for getting out of trouble.

  • Steering clear of revolving credit
  • Keeping track of instalments
  • Protecting yourself from scams
  • 30-day plan
