You see someone interesting and freeze? Confidence in meeting people can be learned, with practice and one step at a time.

Dating with Confidence is a practical e-book in plain language for anyone who wants to meet new people more easily, whether in real life or on dating apps.

You’ll understand the fear of rejection, build confidence in yourself, learn to start and keep conversations going, create a profile that shows who you are, handle the first date, deal with noes and ghosting, and protect yourself from scams. All based on respect for yourself and for the other person.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand and reduce the fear of rejection
  • Start conversations with simple, respectful openers
  • Create a more authentic dating profile and write first messages that get replies
  • Go on a first date with fewer nerves
  • Deal with rejection and ghosting without putting yourself down
  • Stay safe on dates and recognise romance scams

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who freezes when it comes to meeting new people
  • Anyone who’s single again and feels out of practice
  • Anyone who wants to use dating apps with less anxiety

Who it’s not for

  • It is not a manual of “seduction techniques” or manipulation
  • It does not replace therapy for social anxiety

Bonus: Worksheet — a courage ladder to fill in and a date safety checklist, plus a 4-week plan.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
