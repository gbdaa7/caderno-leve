Know what you want to say, but the words get stuck? Shyness can be worked on in small steps, without you having to stop being who you are.

Overcoming Shyness is a practical e-book in plain language for anyone who freezes in conversations, avoids phone calls, stays quiet in meetings or feels out of place at parties.

You’ll understand the difference between shyness, introversion and social anxiety, identify the habits that keep shyness going, question thoughts that hold you back, learn to start and keep conversations going, say what you think and cope in groups, with a ladder of gradual challenges.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand the cycle that keeps shyness going
  • Question thoughts that hold you back
  • Start conversations with simple phrases
  • Keep conversations going with open questions
  • Make requests and give opinions respectfully
  • Build confidence with gradual challenges

What’s inside

  1. Chapter 1Shyness, introversion and social anxiety

  5. Chapter 5Starting conversations

  8. Chapter 8Groups, parties and new situations
  9. Chapter 9Your ladder of challenges
  10. ExtraSummary and action plan to put it into practice
  11. ExtraPrintable worksheet and checklist

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who freezes in conversations and new situations
  • Anyone who avoids phone calls, meetings and parties
  • Anyone who wants to express themselves more, without changing who they are

Who it’s not for

  • Not a treatment for social anxiety
  • Doesn’t promise to turn you into an extrovert

Bonus: printable social courage diary — challenge log and ready-made phrases.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
