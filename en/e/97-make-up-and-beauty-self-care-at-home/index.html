Want to learn to do your make-up without spending much or overcomplicating it? Start with the basics and work up to a complete look.

Make-up and Beauty Self-Care at Home teaches simple techniques for every skin tone, with a lean, affordable kit.

You learn to prepare your skin, choose foundation and concealer, apply blush and highlighter, shape your brows, do eyes from basic to smoky, choose lipsticks, create ready-made looks and take care of product hygiene and shelf life.

  • Hygiene and shelf-life guide
  • Self-care routine

Important notice

This e-book offers general tips for beauty care at home. It is not a substitute for advice from a dermatologist or trichologist. Do a patch test (for allergies) before using new products, follow the label instructions and stop using a product if irritation occurs. No brands are mentioned or recommended.
