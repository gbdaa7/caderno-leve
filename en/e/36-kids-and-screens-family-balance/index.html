Does switching off the tablet turn into a battle in your home? You can find balance with clear agreements and no shouting.

Kids and Screens: Family Balance is a practical e-book in plain language for parents and carers who want to cut screen time for children and teenagers without daily arguments.

You will learn screen time guidelines by age, rethink the example adults set, create a family media plan, learn to handle transitions without a war, offer alternatives to boredom, protect your children online, negotiate with teenagers and know when it’s time to seek help.

What you’ll gain

  • Know the screen time guidelines by age
  • Create a family media plan
  • Switch off screens with fewer arguments
  • Offer creative alternatives
  • Protect children from online risks
  • Negotiate agreements with teenagers

Who it’s for

  • Parents and carers of children and teenagers
  • Families who argue about screens
  • Educators who want to guide families

Who it’s not for

  • Does not replace a paediatrician or psychologist
  • Does not propose banning technology entirely

Bonus: printable family media plan — agreements and weekly check-in.
