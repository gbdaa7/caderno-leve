No time or money for a gym? Train at home with 20-minute routines.

You’ll learn the basic exercises, how to adapt them to your level, three 20-minute routines and a 4-week plan to build the habit.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational purposes and offers general suggestions for physical activity. It is not a substitute for medical assessment or supervision by a qualified fitness professional. If you have heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, pain or injuries, are pregnant or haven't exercised for a long time, talk to a doctor before you start. Stop immediately and seek help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath or severe pain.
