Ever regretted something you said in the heat of the moment? Handling emotions is a skill, and it can be trained.

Emotional Intelligence in Practice is an e-book in plain language, with exercises, for anyone who wants to understand what they feel, react less on impulse and relate better at work and at home.

You’ll learn the pillars of emotional intelligence, widen your emotional vocabulary, notice your body’s signs, use the STOP technique, deal with anger, fear and sadness, practise empathic listening and talk about what bothers you using nonviolent communication.

What you’ll gain

  • Recognise and name emotions more precisely
  • Notice your body’s signs before an outburst
  • Create a pause between feeling and acting
  • Deal with anger, fear, sadness and jealousy in a healthy way
  • Listen with empathy and talk without fighting
  • Apply it all at work and at home

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who reacts on impulse and regrets it later
  • Anyone who wants better relationships at work and at home
  • Parents and leaders who want to teach by example

Who it’s not for

  • Not therapy and not a substitute for psychological support
  • Doesn’t promise to control every emotion

Bonus: printable emotional diary — situation log, 4-step conversation and checklist.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
