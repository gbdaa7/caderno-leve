Do you get anxious when the other person takes a while to reply, do everything to please and dread being alone? This has a name, an explanation and a way forward.

Free from Emotional Dependency is a practical, caring e-book in plain language for anyone who feels their life revolves around someone else and wants to build more balanced relationships.

You’ll understand where this pattern comes from and learn to recognise the dependency cycle, get through the fear of abandonment, strengthen a life of your own and communicate your needs and boundaries, as well as recognise the warning signs of abusive relationships.

What you’ll gain

  • Recognise the signs of emotional dependency and tell them apart from love
  • Understand the roots of the pattern without guilt
  • Identify your cycle: trigger, fear, relief and reinforcement
  • Get through the fear of abandonment with 4 practical steps
  • Strengthen the pillars of a life of your own
  • Make clear requests and practise boundaries

Who it’s for

  • Anyone with an intense fear of being abandoned
  • Anyone who loses themselves in relationships
  • Anyone who wants exercises to complement therapy

Who it’s not for

  • It is not a treatment and does not replace psychotherapy or medical care
  • It is not meant as the only resource in situations of violence: seek specialist support

Bonus: printable worksheet — cycle log, pillars map and daily checklist, plus a 30-day plan.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
