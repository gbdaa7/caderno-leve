Does your heart sink when a notification from the bank arrives? You’re not alone, and you can come out of alarm mode.

At Peace with Money: Financial Anxiety is a practical, supportive e-book in plain language for anyone who lives in fear of bills, avoids checking their balance or loses sleep over debt.

You’ll understand how scarcity affects the mind, learn to look at the numbers calmly, organise an essentials budget, prioritise and negotiate debts safely, learn where to find free debt advice and formal debt solutions, review your beliefs about money, talk to your family and look after your mind during hard times.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand financial anxiety without guilt
  • Look at your real situation calmly and in stages
  • Protect the essentials when money is tight
  • Prioritise and negotiate debts safely
  • Review your beliefs about money
  • Look after your mind while things improve
