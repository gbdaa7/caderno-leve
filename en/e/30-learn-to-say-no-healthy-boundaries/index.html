Say yes to everything and then feel drained and resentful? You can say no respectfully and without guilt.

Learn to Say No: Healthy Boundaries is a practical e-book in plain language for anyone who finds it hard to turn down requests, avoids conflict at all costs or feels their life is organised around other people’s needs.

You’ll understand why saying no is so hard, discover your boundaries, learn the difference between being passive, aggressive and assertive, get ready-made phrases for many situations and see how to apply boundaries with family, at work, with friends and on your phone, while dealing with guilt and other people’s reactions.

What you’ll gain

  • Understand what holds back your no
  • Discover and map your boundaries
  • Communicate assertively
  • Use ready-made phrases to say no respectfully
  • Negotiate priorities at work
  • Deal with guilt and insistence

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who says yes to everything and feels overloaded
  • Anyone who avoids conflict and swallows their feelings
  • Anyone who wants more balanced relationships

Who it’s not for

  • Not therapy and not a substitute for psychological support
  • Not guidance for situations of violence (the e-book points to where you can get help)

Bonus: printable “My boundaries” sheet — conversation planning and a no log.

Important notice

This e-book is for educational and self-care purposes. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for therapy or medical care. If your distress is intense, lasts a long time or gets in the way of your life, please reach out to a psychologist, a doctor or your local health service.

If you need to talk to someone right now, contact a local crisis line or helpline. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
